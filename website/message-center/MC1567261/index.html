OneDrive for Business: Bug fixes to ensure proper storage quota aligns with license limits

24 November 2026MC1567261 on mc.merill.net ↗Message

In brief

OneDrive for Business will fix a bug causing incorrect 1 TB storage quotas for users with certain license combinations. Starting January 2027, quotas will match the actual storage plan, potentially reducing some users' quotas. Organizations should review usage, adjust licenses, and prepare for possible read-only states.

Message Center announcement

What and Why:

We're fixing an issue in OneDrive for Business license evaluation so that a user's OneDrive storage quota is determined by their applicable storage plan.

Today, users assigned certain storage-providing plans in combination with certain non-storage plans can incorrectly receive a 1 TB OneDrive quota. After this change, these combinations will no longer result in a 1 TB quota. OneDrive will instead apply the quota associated with the user's storage plan, which may be lower than 1 TB.

This change improves license and storage entitlement accuracy, helping organizations manage OneDrive capacity consistently with their assigned licenses.

Rollout Schedule:

Global: We will begin rolling out in early January 2027 and expect to complete by late January 2027.

Impact on Your Organization:

Who is affected: Your organization may be affected if users have a plan from both lists below.

Plans that provide OneDrive storage:

  • Deskless: 2 GB
  • Basic or Basic P3: 5 GB
  • Basic P2 or Plan 2A: 10 GB
  • OneDrive Clipchamp: 100 GB

Plans that don't provide OneDrive storage but, when combined with one of the plans above, can currently result in an incorrect 1 TB quota:

  • M365 Copilot SharePoint
  • Microsoft 365 Copilot Stream
  • Microsoft Syntex Seat
  • Viva Amplify
  • Microsoft Clipchamp
  • Microsoft Clipchamp Premium
  • Viva Connections Premium

Users with these license-plan combinations are incorrectly assigned the standard 1 TB OneDrive quota instead of the storage allowance provided by their storage plan.

What will happen: OneDrive will correct how storage entitlement is determined for the license-plan combinations listed above. The user's OneDrive quota will instead reflect their applicable storage plan (as listed above). As a result, affected users who currently have a 1 TB quota will see their OneDrive storage quota decrease.

If an affected user is consuming more storage than their corrected license quota allows, their OneDrive will enter a read-only state. Users can reduce their stored content to bring their usage within their licensed quota. Organizations can also purchase additional storage using capacity packs.

Action Required/Recommendations:

  • Review OneDrive storage usage across your organization to identify users whose usage exceeds their licensed quota. See Identify OneDrive Users Over License-Based Storage Quota | PnP Samples and OneDrive site is read-only because storage quota was exceeded - SharePoint | Microsoft Learn.
  • Take corrective action for affected users: upgrade the user's license to increase available storage, or work with the user to reduce storage usage to within licensed limits.
  • Inform your helpdesk and SharePoint admins about this change so they can support users who may be placed into a read-only state.
  • If all users are within their licensed storage quotas, no action is required.

Additional resources: Add storage space, Site storage overquota enforcement FAQ.

Compliance considerations:

No compliance considerations identified. Review as appropriate for your organization.
