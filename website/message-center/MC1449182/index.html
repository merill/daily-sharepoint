Microsoft 365 Copilot: Copilot Pages and Notebooks Creation in OneDrive

14 November 2026MC1449182 on mc.merill.net ↗Message

In brief

Starting mid-October 2026, newly created Microsoft 365 Copilot Pages and Notebooks will be stored in OneDrive for Business, enabling existing governance, compliance, data protection, retention, and lifecycle management features to apply. Existing files remain unchanged; no user action is required.

Message Center announcement

Updated November 13, 2026: We have updated the timeline. Thank you for your patience. 

What and why

We're changing where newly created Copilot Pages are stored. Going forward, Copilot Pages created in Copilot Chat, the Microsoft 365 Copilot app, and new Copilot Notebooks will be stored in OneDrive for Business instead of their current location.

This change enables organizations to manage newly created Copilot Pages using existing OneDrive for Business governance, compliance, data protection, retention, and lifecycle management capabilities.

Rollout schedule

  • General Availability (Worldwide): Beginning in mid-October 2026 (previously mid-August) and expected to complete by late November 2026 (previously late September)
  • General Availability (GCC, GCC High, and DoD): Beginning in mid-November 2026 (previously mid-September) and expected to complete by mid-December 2026 (previously early December)

Impact on your organization

Who is affected

Users who create Copilot Pages in:

  • Copilot Chat
  • Microsoft 365 Copilot app
  • Copilot Notebooks

Microsoft 365 administrators responsible for governance, compliance, retention, and content management

Platforms and services

  • Microsoft 365 Copilot
  • Copilot Chat
  • Copilot Notebooks
  • OneDrive for Business
  • Microsoft Purview compliance solutions

What will happen

After rollout:

  • Newly created Copilot Pages will be stored in users' OneDrive for Business.
  • New Copilot Pages will appear alongside other OneDrive files, such as Word, Excel, and PowerPoint documents.
  • New Copilot Notebooks and artifacts created within them will appear alongside other OneDrive files, such as Word, Excel, and PowerPoint documents.
  • Existing OneDrive governance, compliance, data protection, retention, lifecycle management, reporting, and investigation capabilities will apply to newly created Copilot Pages.

In addition to all existing governance and management capabilities, the following additional OneDrive for Business capabilities will apply to newly created Copilot Pages:

Compliance and legal

  • Standard file path-based eDiscovery
  • Retention labels with record locking
  • Information Barriers
  • Compliance Boundaries

Data protection

  • Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies and mitigation workflows
  • Third-party governance solutions that support OneDrive content

Lifecycle management

  • Retention and lifecycle policies
  • Existing departed-user workflows for OneDrive content

Monitoring and oversight

  • OneDrive storage reporting
  • Administrative investigations of content stored in OneDrive

Unaffected files

  • Existing Copilot Pages will remain in their current location and will not be migrated as part of this change.
  • Existing Copilot Notebooks will continue to store all newly created pages in SharePoint Embedded. Only Copilot Notebooks created after this change will store pages in OneDrive. 
  • Loop workspaces are not affected and will continue to use their current storage location.

IT Admin controls

  • If your organization has set Create and view Copilot Pages and Copilot Notebooks to Disabled, that setting will continue to be honored after this change.
  • This admin control is planned for future retirement. Microsoft will provide additional communication and transition guidance before the setting is retired.

Action required and recommendations

No action is required for the rollout.

We recommend that administrators:

  • Review existing DLP, retention, and compliance policies that apply to OneDrive for Business.
  • Verify that any third-party governance or eDiscovery solutions are configured to monitor OneDrive content as appropriate.
  • Update internal documentation and governance guidance for AI-generated content.
  • Communicate to users that newly created Copilot Pages will be stored in their OneDrive for Business.

Compliance considerations

QuestionAnswer
Does this change alter where customer data is stored?Yes. Newly created Copilot Pages will be stored in OneDrive for Business instead of their previous storage location.
Does this change affect compliance and governance capabilities?Yes. Newly created Copilot Pages will be subject to OneDrive for Business compliance capabilities, including eDiscovery, retention labels with record locking, Information Barriers, and Compliance Boundaries.
Does this change affect data protection controls?Yes. Existing OneDrive for Business data protection controls, including Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies and supported third-party governance solutions, can be applied to newly created Copilot Pages.
Does this change affect lifecycle management and retention processes?Yes. Existing OneDrive for Business retention policies, lifecycle management capabilities, and departed-user workflows will apply to newly created Copilot Pages.
Does this change affect how administrators monitor or investigate content?Yes. Administrators will be able to use standard OneDrive for Business reporting, monitoring, and investigation capabilities for newly created Copilot Pages.
