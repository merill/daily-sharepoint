Campaigns in SharePoint

25 November 2026MC1568699 on mc.merill.net ↗Message

In brief

SharePoint is introducing site campaigns, integrating Viva Amplify's campaign management directly into SharePoint for unified creation, distribution, and analytics of news posts. Available to both seeded and licensed Viva Amplify users, rollout begins mid-August 2026. No changes to existing Amplify campaigns or licensing.

Message Center announcement

What and Why:

Today we're sharing an update to the new/upcoming SharePoint Publish experience: campaigns in SharePoint (site campaigns). This new capability brings Viva Amplify's campaign management natively into SharePoint, giving corporate communications teams a unified place to create, distribute, and measure their news without leaving the platform where their news content already lives.

This feature applies to both seeded SharePoint users and premium users licensed for Viva Amplify. Amplify users can continue to create Amplify app campaigns as they see fit. This new feature provides a complementary way to create lighter weight campaigns inside a SharePoint site as a way to organize your SharePoint news posts and benefit from campaign analytics directly from SharePoint. There are no changes to Viva Amplify's license model. Note: Amplify 'create campaign' settings in the admin center only apply to Amplify app campaigns.

Rollout Schedule:

Preview (if applicable): Not Applicable

Targeted Release (if applicable): Late July to August 2026

Standard Release (if applicable): August - Sept 2026

General Availability (Worldwide): We will begin rolling out on mid August 2026 and expect to complete by end of November 2026.

Impact on Your Organization:

Who is affected:

• SharePoint and Viva Amplify users and corporate communications teams that publish and manage SharePoint news.

Platforms/Services:

• SharePoint Online, Viva Amplify.

What will happen:

  • Amplify users can continue to create and manage campaigns in the Viva Amplify app.
  • A new SharePoint experience will allow users to create lightweight site campaigns directly within SharePoint.
  • Site campaigns can be used to organize related SharePoint news posts, and publish these news posts across SharePoint, Viva Engage, Teams and Outlook Email.
  • Users will have access to campaign analytics from within SharePoint.
  • There are no changes to the existing Viva Amplify campaign experience.
  • The feature is available to both seeded SharePoint users and premium users licensed for Viva Amplify.

Action Required/Recommendations:

There is no action for admins beyond reviewing any internal help or guidance you may have created for end users.

  • Review internal documentation and prepare end-user guidance as appropriate.
  • Communicate the availability of SharePoint site campaigns to appropriate SharePoint users, Viva Amplify users, corporate communications and content publishing teams.
  • Be aware that the Viva Amplify Create campaign admin setting applies only to campaigns created in the Viva Amplify app and does not govern SharePoint site campaigns.

Compliance considerations:

No compliance considerations identified, review as appropriate for your organization.
