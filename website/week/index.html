Week in brief

Purview priority deletion and archive controls expand as classic SharePoint restrictions approach

Microsoft 365 notices point to two consequential governance changes: a Purview priority-cleanup workflow is scheduled for general availability in early December 2026, while SharePoint Online will begin blocking creation and activation of classic publishing sites and features on March 1, 2027. Purview also plans retention-based archiving for inactive SharePoint files; the period’s Trim Versions guidance now specifies a 30-day data-collection results window.

  • SharePoint · General
    Priority cleanup will hard-delete selected SharePoint and OneDrive content

    Microsoft Purview Data Lifecycle Management is introducing a priority cleanup workflow that can hard-delete specific OneDrive and SharePoint content while overriding retention policies. The workflow requires explicit administrator setup and eDiscovery administrator approval; the notice places preview rollout in mid-August 2026 and general availability in early December 2026.

  • SharePoint · Permissions and governance
    Classic publishing creation and activation will be blocked

    From March 1, 2027, SharePoint Online will block creation and activation of classic publishing sites and features, while enforcing restrictions on custom scripting. New tenants face additional limits on classic pages, and administrators are expected to update workflows and modernize pages before rollout completes by October 2028.

  • SharePoint · Permissions and governance
    Retention policies will support archiving inactive SharePoint files

    Microsoft Purview Data Lifecycle Management will let administrators archive inactive SharePoint files under retention policies starting mid-January 2027. Archived files move to Microsoft 365 Archive, remain compliant and discoverable, and are excluded from Copilot indexing; administrator configuration is required.

  • Content and versions · General
    Trim Versions guidance defines a 30-day data-collection window

    The updated guidance says data-collection results are retained for 30 days after a job completes. After that window, a new data-collection job is needed to perform what-if analysis, so collection must be rerun when later analysis is required.

For SharePoint and OneDrive administrators

Administrators should prepare workflow and page modernization ahead of the March 1, 2027 classic-publishing restriction. For Purview, plan explicit setup for priority cleanup, an eDiscovery administrator approval path for hard deletes, and configuration for SharePoint archiving under retention policies. Trim Versions users need a new data-collection job to run what-if analysis after the 30-day results-retention window.
