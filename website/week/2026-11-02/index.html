Week in brief

Unified Copilot Search and group-permission governance reporting headline SharePoint administration

Two Message Center announcements carry the period: SharePoint Online and OneDrive for the web are moving to a unified Copilot Search experience, with preview rollout beginning in mid-October 2026 and general rollout in mid-January 2027; SharePoint’s Data access governance report for group permissions is scheduled to roll out worldwide from late November to early December. A separate Agents and Copilot article now documents the full site-skills management workflow.

  • SharePoint · AI and Copilot
    Copilot Search unifies SharePoint Online and OneDrive web search

    The updated experience adds natural-language prompts, AI summaries, and filters. The announced schedule places preview rollout from mid-October 2026 and general rollout from mid-January 2027; users can opt out during preview and organizational action is minimal.

  • SharePoint · Reports and usage
    Data access governance report adds group-permission visibility

    The new report in the SharePoint admin center will help administrators identify sites accessible to specific groups and assess their access levels. Worldwide rollout is scheduled from late November to early December 2026, supporting permission management and efforts to prevent unintended content exposure.

  • Agents and Copilot · AI and Copilot
    Site skills management is documented under Settings > Site skills

    The updated article explains how to create and manage site skills, including uploading, editing, enabling, disabling, copying, restoring, downloading, and deleting them. It also clarifies storage, ownership, and permissions, giving site owners a documented management path.

For SharePoint and OneDrive administrators

Copilot Search requires minimal organizational action, and users can opt out during preview. Administrators can use the forthcoming report in the admin center to find sites accessible to particular groups and assess access levels, supporting permission reviews and checks for unintended content exposure. Site owners can administer skills from Settings > Site skills using the documented controls; the article states that no action is required.
