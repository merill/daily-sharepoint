Month in brief

Unified Copilot Search and group-permission reporting lead November SharePoint changes

November brings two consequential SharePoint announcements: a unified Copilot Search experience for SharePoint Online and OneDrive for the web, and a Data access governance report for group permissions. A separate Agents and Copilot article documents the full site-skills management workflow, including its controls, storage, ownership, and permissions.

  • SharePoint · AI and Copilot
    Unified Copilot Search moves SharePoint and OneDrive toward one search experience

    Microsoft announced an updated web search experience combining SharePoint Online and OneDrive with natural-language prompts, AI summaries, and filters. Preview rollout began in mid-October 2026, with general rollout scheduled for mid-January 2027; users can opt out during preview.

  • SharePoint · Reports and usage
    Group-permission governance report is coming to the SharePoint admin center

    The new Data access governance report will help administrators identify sites accessible to specific groups and assess those groups’ access levels, supporting permission reviews and checks for unintended content exposure. Worldwide rollout is scheduled from late November to early December 2026.

  • Agents and Copilot · AI and Copilot
    Site-skills guidance now covers the complete management workflow

    The updated Copilot In SharePoint Skills article documents managing site skills from Settings > Site skills, including uploading, editing, enabling, disabling, copying, restoring, downloading, and deleting them. It also clarifies storage, ownership, and permissions; site owners can use these documented controls without a required administrative action.

For SharePoint and OneDrive administrators

Copilot Search is scheduled for preview from mid-October 2026 and general rollout from mid-January 2027; organizations need minimal action, and users can opt out during preview. The governance report, rolling out worldwide from late November to early December 2026, will support reviews of which sites specific groups can access and at what access levels. Site owners can manage skills under Settings > Site skills, with no action required based on the supplied guidance.
