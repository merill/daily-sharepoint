Month in brief

Classic publishing deadline, Purview hard-delete, and agent-access preview reshape SharePoint

November brought a major SharePoint governance push alongside two important deadlines. SharePoint Online and OneDrive for the web are moving to unified Copilot Search with natural-language prompts, AI summaries, and filters, while a preview agent-access heatmap helps administrators investigate agents reaching sensitive content. Purview’s priority cleanup workflow is scheduled for general availability in early December, enabling approved hard deletion despite retention policies. SharePoint will block creation and activation of classic publishing sites and features from March 1, 2027, and temporary authentication URLs will retire April 1, 2027, breaking dependent Power Apps and Power Automate functionality.

  • SharePoint · Permissions and governance
    Classic publishing restrictions get a March 2027 enforcement date

    From March 1, 2027, SharePoint Online will block creation and activation of classic publishing sites and features and enforce restrictions on custom scripting. New tenants face additional classic-page limits, with the broader rollout completing by October 2028. Administrators should update affected workflows and modernize pages.

  • SharePoint · General
    Purview priority cleanup moves hard deletion toward general availability

    The priority cleanup workflow is scheduled for general availability in early December 2026. It allows administrators to hard-delete selected OneDrive and SharePoint content while overriding retention policies, but requires explicit setup and eDiscovery administrator approval.

  • Agents and Copilot · AI and Copilot
    Agent-access heatmap preview maps exposure to sensitive content

    A preview report in the SharePoint admin center lets administrators investigate agents reaching sensitive content. The documented view requires specified licensing and administrator permissions; sensitivity labels and business-group configuration affect how complete the heatmap is.

  • SharePoint · AI and Copilot
    Copilot Search unifies SharePoint and OneDrive web search

    SharePoint Online and OneDrive for the web are moving to a unified Copilot Search experience with natural-language prompts, AI-generated summaries, and filters. Preview rollout began in mid-October 2026, with general rollout scheduled for mid-January 2027; users can opt out during preview and organizations need minimal preparation.

  • SharePoint · General
    SharePoint temporary authentication URLs retire April 1, 2027

    SharePoint temporary authentication URLs will no longer be available from April 1, 2027. Power Apps and Power Automate functionality that relies on these URLs will break, making dependency identification a priority for affected integrations.

For SharePoint and OneDrive administrators

Inventory classic publishing sites, features, custom scripting, and dependent workflows before March 1, 2027, and identify Power Apps and Power Automate dependencies on temporary authentication URLs before April 1, 2027. Prepare the required Purview setup and eDiscovery approval process for priority cleanup. For the agent-access heatmap, confirm licensing and administrator permissions and recognize that sensitivity labels and business-group configuration affect coverage. Copilot Search requires minimal organizational action, and users can opt out during preview.
